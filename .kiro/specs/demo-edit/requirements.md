# Requirements: demo video edit

1. THE final video SHALL be at most 4:10 and 2560x1440.
2. THE SYSTEM SHALL cut the talk from word timestamps (Whisper) and SHALL NOT cut in the middle of a sentence.
3. EVERY cut SHALL snap to the quietest 10 ms within 0.2 s and use a 0.3 s dissolve (no hard cuts).
4. THE screen recordings SHALL be cropped so no window title bar or browser chrome shows.
5. THE edit SHALL show: trailer intro, section cards 01 to 05, centered captions (max 5 words), landing page QR + link, Instagram proof card, both explainer clips, an end LINKS panel with GitHub, website and docs QR codes.
6. THE explainer clips SHALL NOT overlap the proof card.
7. THE music SHALL duck under the voice. THE final mix SHALL be -16 LUFS.
