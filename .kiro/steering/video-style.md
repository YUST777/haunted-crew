# Video style

- Minecraft theme everywhere. Titles in the Minecraft font, UI text in Inter.
- Colors: charcoal background, white glow, ONE teal accent. No purple, no gradients.
- Ghosts are always solid, never transparent.
- No hard cuts: dissolves of 0.3 s.
- Never show a window title bar, browser chrome or a notification pop-up.
- Captions: centered, max 5 words per line, fixed position.
